# My Favorite Movie: As Above, So Below

**As Above, So Below** is a 2014 found-footage horror film that follows a team of explorers through the catacombs beneath Paris.

## About the Film
- **Director:** John Erick Dowdle
- **Release Year:** 2014
- **Genre:** Found-Footage Horror, Psychological Thriller
- **Starring:** Perdita Weeks, Ben Feldman, Edwin Hodge

## Plot Summary
A young archaeologist and her team venture into the Paris catacombs in search of the legendary Philosopher's Stone, only to find themselves trapped in a nightmarish version of hell that mirrors their past sins.

## Why I Love This Movie
- Unique blend of historical mystery and supernatural horror
- Claustrophobic atmosphere in the real Paris catacombs
- Clever incorporation of alchemical philosophy and Dante's Inferno
- The psychological depth where characters face their personal demons
- Smart, capable female lead who drives the narrative

## Memorable Concept
> **"As above, so below"** - The central alchemical principle that the macrocosm reflects the microcosm

## Rating
I give this movie **4.5/5 stars** for its intelligent horror and unique premise.
